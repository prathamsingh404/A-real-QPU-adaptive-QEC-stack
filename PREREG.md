# Internal analysis protocol for the causal recalibration study

The November 2026 audit informed this protocol. This is an internal, versioned design. The older simulation and correction history are archived under archive/study_v2. Freeze the code and YAML before opening evaluation outputs. Preflight uses seed 70000 and has no role in inference.

The primary contrast is selective minus periodic logical error rate (LER) in the d=3 Z-memory hotspot at W=2000. Both methods may rebuild at most twice and use identical 50:50 shrinkage. The periodic schedule rebuilds at one-third and two-thirds of the trajectory. The selective policy rebuilds on entry to a detector alarm, retains its graph during sustained alarms and returns to the factory graph when the alarm clears. Record actual rebuild and reset counts. A common cap does not ensure equal realized compute.

A favorable conclusion requires a negative mean paired difference with a two-sided Holm-adjusted paired t-test below 0.05 across the four d=3 Z-memory scenarios at W=2000, AND an upper one-sided 95% bound below +0.0006 for selective minus static LER on the stationary scenario. The minimum meaningful hotspot improvement is 0.0006 absolute. Report paired t intervals and signed-rank sensitivity. All-zero differences receive p=1 and a degenerate empirical t interval, which cannot prove zero population risk. Other distances, basis, windows, comparisons and public hardware replay are descriptive. The small secondary panels cannot establish thresholds or universal safety.

Every seed has an independent nominal 6000-shot calibration. A trajectory contains 60 batches of 200 independent memory shots. The noise law varies between batches. Onset is drawn uniformly from block indices 12 through 20 and duration from 16 through 28. The hotspot location and amplitude vary by seed. Uniform changes ramp over six blocks at each end. Localized and biased Pauli channels are inserted at every flattened tick. The same stream is reused at both W values. Ground-truth labels score predictions after decoding. Only the matched-model reference knows the current physical model. The seven deployable arms receive completed unlabeled batches. Fit and reset actions affect the following batch.

Detector alarms use twice the smaller hypergeometric tail conditional on the combined reference and current counts. Bonferroni covers all detectors and all planned looks under identical independent stationary shots and an independent reference sample. This controls false alarms under those assumptions. It gives no confidence bound on a change in LER. Empirical cost measurements must time the actual operations separately from shared benchmark caching. No hardware feedback latency is inferred. The archived simulation, public experimental file-order replay, and independent simulations have different sampling units.
