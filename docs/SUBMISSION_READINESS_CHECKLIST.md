# Submission assessment

The frozen primary contrast failed its effect-size and corrected significance criteria. The current manuscript can support a comparative benchmark or negative-result discussion. A stronger decoder or device paper needs a competitive complete external algorithm, an independently acquired time-ordered test, and evidence that the measured advantage survives those comparisons.
