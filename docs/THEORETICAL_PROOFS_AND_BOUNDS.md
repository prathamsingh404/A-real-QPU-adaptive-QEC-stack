# Scope of the statistical argument

Under independent shots and constant detector marginals shared by the reference and evaluation samples, conditioning on the combined count gives a hypergeometric null for one detector. Twice the smaller tail is conservative. A union bound over detectors and planned looks controls the probability of any false alarm at the configured level, even though detectors and tests share samples. Correlated shots and drifting calibration can invalidate the null model.

This argument concerns detector alarms. Decoder accuracy, regret, physical threshold and feedback latency each require separate evidence. An additive offset per edge can change the ordering of paths with different lengths. The historical general invariance and regret claims are withdrawn. [Tested protocol](../PREREG.md)
