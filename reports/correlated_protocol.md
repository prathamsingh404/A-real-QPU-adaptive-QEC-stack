# Prospective protocol for correlated matching updates

This internal protocol follows the original timing study and its inspected outcomes. Development used eight distance-three seeds per regime and 8,000 shots per trajectory. Reweighting detector-error mechanisms with local or global marginal information had little hindsight headroom. We therefore also developed a library of correlated graphs obtained by injecting a fixed two-percent local fault at one candidate data qubit per circuit tick. All graphs preserve DEM component separators and logical fault targets. The library explicitly knows the class of candidate noise changes. Template selection uses only the previous window's detector marginals. The validation loss for the empirical-prior rule is a marginal composite likelihood, and its penalties are tuning constants.

Freeze source, development choices and this protocol in Git before computing evaluation results. No fresh QPU acquisition. Seed ranges in the evaluation script are disjoint from development. Each window contains 1,000 complete independent memory shots; actions affect exactly the next window. Compare seven policies on common input streams. The always-full control refreshes every window. Factory, correlation-preserving empirical local/full selection, calibrated calendar full refresh, and three cached-library look schedules complete the comparison. Calendar times are chosen by minimizing development error; a cached graph used for another window counts as another use. Both selective primary policies permit at most two nonfactory windows per trajectory.

Two primary tests at d=3 compare (1) selective empirical updating vs its development-trained calendar and (2) budgeted library selection vs its same-library trained calendar. Pool equal numbers of independent episodes under stationary, abrupt, gradual and recurring mechanisms, 24 per condition. Use paired two-sided t tests with Holm across both primary hypotheses, pointwise 95% intervals, negative differences favorable. Practical success requires adjusted p below .05, mean improvement at least .0006 absolute and aggregate measured online time at most the comparator's. Evaluate compute as an observed budget criterion, with equal caps on actions and no hardware deadline. Report cold library compilation separately. The time samples include decode and decision/build followed by the next window's use; exclude acquisition, label scoring and initialization. Randomize order within arm families. Local masks restrict the changed probabilities; the underlying library still builds a full matching graph.

Distance five, uniform and biased shifts are transfer checks, including changes outside the fault library. Physical tests use the lexicographically first two d=3 patches, both bases, 10 rounds, traditional calibration in Zenodo 18896801. Apply the frozen simulation choices without tuning. Each 100,000-shot record contributes twelve independent policy restarts of eight windows and omits the last 4,000 shots for fixed horizon. File segments are descriptive and share physical provenance. The published schema provides no verified chronological sequence across shots or bounded drift episodes. All physical performance numbers refer to offline file-order replay. Treat clock readings as host-specific. Record each competing policy's losses, updates, actions and timings regardless of sign.
