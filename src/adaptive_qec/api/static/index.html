The Fundamental Challenge

Why Do Quantum Computers Need Adaptation?

Classical computers use billions of transistors that rarely make mistakes. Quantum computers use delicate superconducting transmon circuits where noise is non-stationary—it changes by the second.

⚡

1. Qubits Drift Constantly

Cosmic rays, microscopic two-level fluctuators in the chip substrate, and cryogenic temperature ripples cause transmon frequencies to shift over time. A gate calibration that worked at 9:00 AM becomes noisy by 9:15 AM.

⚠️

2. Static QEC Blindness

Traditional quantum error correction (QEC) decoders assume noise is static and fixed. When noise spikes or drifts, static decoders misinterpret error syndromes and accidentally inject logical bit-flips into the computation.

🛡️

3. The Adaptive Solution

AdaptiveQEC continuously monitors live hardware telemetry. When it detects drift, it automatically switches dynamical decoupling schedules, recalibrates error models, and resolves errors in under 10 microseconds.
